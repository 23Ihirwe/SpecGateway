How should I use my SpecGateway result? +

Use your result as a starting point for reflection. SpecGateway identifies patterns across your answers, but it should not replace academic advice, personal exploration, or your own judgement.

Can I take the assessment again? +

Yes. You can retake the assessment, especially after learning more about Low-Level Programming, AR/VR, Full-Stack Web Development, and Machine Learning.

Does SpecGateway store my answers? +

SpecGateway processes your assessment information in the browser. Browser local storage is used only when information needs to move between the assessment and results pages.

Who built SpecGateway? +

SpecGateway was created by Hildegardine Ihirwe as a BSE Specialisation Advisor designed to help incoming software engineering students reflect on their interests, strengths, and preferred ways of solving problems.
